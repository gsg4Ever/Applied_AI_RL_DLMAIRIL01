Core explanation

Experience replay stores and reuses state-action-reward-successor tuples; randomized sampling reduces temporal correlations, and prioritized replay concentrates training on experiences expected to improve predictions most.

Active recall

Answer before expanding. Open the answers before printing to include them.

What does an experience tuple contain?

A state, an action, the reward and the successor state.

Why store a replay buffer?

To reuse interaction data and train on more than the latest experience.

Why randomly sample from replay?

To reduce correlation among consecutive training experiences.

What does uniform replay ignore?

Differences in rarity, informativeness and remaining learning value.

What motivates prioritized replay in the book?

Experiences with large remaining prediction discrepancies offer more learning progress.

What does the priority exponent control?

The strength of the prioritization in the sampling probabilities.
