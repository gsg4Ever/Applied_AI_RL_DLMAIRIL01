Core explanation

Model-free learning uses experience directly: Monte Carlo prediction averages complete episode returns, while TD learns from reward-plus-continuation estimates and can support stepwise control.

Key terms

TermMeaning
Model-free predictionEstimate values under a given policy without an explicit model.
Model-free controlImprove action choice directly from experience.

ReturnCumulative, possibly discounted, future reward.

Active recall

Answer before expanding. Open the answers before printing to include them.

What does a model-free method avoid building first?

An explicit environment model of transition probabilities and rewards.

What is the difference between prediction and control?

Prediction evaluates a policy; control improves action selection.

What does every visit mean in Monte Carlo evaluation?

Include the return from each occurrence of a state, including repeated visits in one episode.

What is averaged to estimate a state value?

The subsequent returns associated with visits to that state.

Why does the presented Monte Carlo procedure need termination?

It must observe a complete episode to obtain its full returns.

How can TD update earlier?

It bootstraps a continuation estimate after the next reward and state.

How does SARSA's successor target differ from Q-learning's?

SARSA uses the policy-selected next action; Q-learning uses the maximum next-state Q-value.
