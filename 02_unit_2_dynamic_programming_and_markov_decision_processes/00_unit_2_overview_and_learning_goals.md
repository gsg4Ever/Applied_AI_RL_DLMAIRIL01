# Unit 2: Dynamic Programming and Markov Decision Processes

## Learning goals from the course book

- Precisely formulate sequential decision processes.
- Determine and apply their optimal decision rules.
- Explain how the optimization principles are implemented in programs.
- Explain the role of utility theory in economic applications.

## Introduction and connections

The unit formalizes states, admissible actions, transitions, policies and value functions. The stagecoach and treasure-search examples develop Bellman's recursion; utility and minimax criteria show why the definition of performance matters.
