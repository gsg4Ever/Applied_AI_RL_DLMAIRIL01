# Unit 1: Introduction to Reinforcement Learning

## Learning goals from the course book

- Identify the problems solved by reinforcement learning.
- Explain which obstacles of traditional control theory reinforcement learning addresses.
- Recognize the main methods and components of reinforcement learning.

## Introduction and connections

Sequential decisions affect accumulated cost or reward. This unit moves from classical control and dynamic programming to learning from agent-environment interaction, and introduces Monte Carlo evaluation, TD methods, Q-learning, SARSA, policy improvement, exploration and neural-network approximation.
